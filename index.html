Interactive Question Prompts

1. What happens to c when a increases?

Answer: The hypotenuse c increases as well because the legs become longer.

2. Which side is the hypotenuse?

Answer: The longest side, which is always directly opposite the 90° right angle.

3. Why can we use Pythagoras' theorem here?

Answer: Because the triangle is right-angled. (It never applies to non-right-angled triangles).

Remember — Step-by-Step Problem Solving

Step 1 Check that the triangle is right-angled.
Step 2 Identify the hypotenuse opposite 90°.
Step 3 Write down a² + b² = c².
Step 4 Substitute the known values of a and b.
Step 5 Solve for the missing side c.
Step 6 Check that your answer makes sense (c > a and c > b).
